The ``encodings`` package and the codecs that every interpreter imports at startup (``encodings.aliases``, ``encodings.utf_8`` and the platform codecs) are frozen again, which makes interpreter startup faster. The other codecs are still imported from the standard library: when the directory of a frozen package is not available, for example because the standard library is zipped, its ``__path__`` is now resolved through the path-based finders so that its non-frozen submodules can be imported.
